## Site Information

The site is one of the first sites setup when building the backbone back in 2021. This site provides coverage to a large part of Paso Robles and south towards Templeton. There are also a number of repeaters hosted here as well as a fully functional  telephone switching system. You can learn more about them at [w6rr.com](https://w6rr.com/)
